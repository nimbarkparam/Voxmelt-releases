# Voxmelt Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Version:** 2.1
**Effective date:** 13 July 2026
**Applies to:** Pro and Studio subscriptions to Voxmelt.

---

## The short version

You get a **15-day full-feature trial, no card required**, so you can confirm
Voxmelt actually runs on your machine *before* you pay a cent. Because you can fully
evaluate the app for free first, **all paid charges are final - purchases are
non-refundable.** You can **cancel anytime in one click**: that stops future
renewals, and at the end of the cycle you have already paid for your account
automatically reverts to the free tier. That's the whole deal.

---

## 1. Try before you buy

Every fresh install of Voxmelt includes a **free 15-day trial of all features**
(the full Studio experience), with **no payment method required** and no
obligation to subscribe. The trial is anchored to your device, so reinstalling
on the same machine doesn't reset it. You are **never** charged automatically
when a trial ends - a paid subscription begins **only** when you explicitly
subscribe at **voxmelt.com/pricing**.

This trial exists specifically so you can verify, on your own hardware, that
Voxmelt installs, downloads its models, detects your GPU if you have one, and
transcribes to your satisfaction before any money changes hands.

## 2. No refunds - all sales are final

Because the free 15-day trial gives you a full opportunity to evaluate every
paid feature on your own hardware before paying, **paid subscription charges are
non-refundable.** Once a charge is made for a billing cycle it **cannot be
refunded** - whether it is your first payment or a later renewal. This applies
equally to Indian and international customers.

By starting a paid subscription you confirm that you have had a full opportunity
to evaluate the paid features during the free trial and you accept this
no-refund policy. To stop being charged in the future, cancel before your
renewal date (see §3).

## 3. Cancel anytime - one click

You can cancel from **voxmelt.com/account/billing** with a single "Cancel"
action. No email, no phone call, no "are you sure" gauntlet. Cancellation:

- Stops **future** renewals only.
- Leaves your full Pro/Studio access intact **through the end of the billing
  cycle you have already paid for**.
- On that expiry date, **automatically reverts your account to the free tier**,
  with no further charges.

There is **no refund** for the unused remainder of a cancelled cycle - you
simply keep full access until it ends, then drop to free.

## 4. Failed renewal payments

If a renewal payment fails (for example, an expired card or insufficient
balance), Razorpay retries the charge on its standard schedule and your account
stays on the paid tier during that short retry window. If all retries fail, the
account **automatically reverts to the free tier**. Your local data on the
desktop app is never deleted - update your payment method and resubscribe to
regain paid access.

## 5. Chargebacks & disputes

If you believe a charge is incorrect or unauthorised, please email
**help@voxmelt.com** *before* raising a chargeback with your bank. We aim to
respond within 2 business days and resolve billing issues quickly. Raising a
chargeback without contacting us first may result in account termination and
forfeiture of any unused subscription period.

## 6. Your statutory rights

This no-refund policy does **not** override any **non-waivable** refund or
withdrawal rights you may have under the mandatory consumer law where you live.
Where such rights apply and cannot be waived, they take precedence. If you think
you have such a right, email us and we'll sort it out.

## 7. Contact

Billing or cancellation questions: **help@voxmelt.com**
