# Voxmelt Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective date:** 2 June 2026
**Applies to:** Pro and Studio subscriptions to Voxmelt.

---

## The short version

You get a **15-day full-feature trial, no card required**, so you can confirm
Voxmelt actually runs on your GPU *before* you pay a cent. Because you've had a
real chance to try everything, paid charges are **non-refundable once a billing
cycle starts** - but you can **cancel future renewals anytime in one click**,
and if our service has a long outage, we'll make it right. That's the whole
deal.

---

## 1. Try before you buy

Every fresh install of Voxmelt includes a **free 15-day trial of all features**
(the full Studio experience), with **no payment method required** and no
obligation to subscribe. The trial is anchored to your device, so reinstalling
on the same machine doesn't reset it. You will **never** be charged
automatically when a trial ends - a paid subscription begins **only** when you
explicitly subscribe at **voxmelt.com/pricing**.

This trial exists specifically so you can verify, on your own hardware, that
Voxmelt installs, detects your GPU, downloads its models, and transcribes to
your satisfaction before any money changes hands.

## 2. Cancel anytime - one click

You can cancel from **voxmelt.com/account/billing** with a single "Cancel"
action. No email, no phone call, no "are you sure" gauntlet. Cancellation:

- Stops **future** renewals only.
- Leaves your full Pro/Studio access intact **through the end of the billing
  cycle you've already paid for**.
- Reverts your account to the free tier afterwards, with **no further charges**.

To avoid the next charge, just cancel before your renewal date.

## 3. Refunds

Because the free trial gives you a full opportunity to evaluate Voxmelt before
paying, **paid subscription charges are non-refundable once a billing cycle has
begun** - whether that's your first payment or a later renewal. Cancelling
stops the *next* charge (see §2); it does not refund a cycle already started.

By subscribing, you confirm you've had the chance to evaluate the paid features
during the free trial and you accept this no-refund policy.

## 4. The exception: a sustained Voxmelt-side outage

The no-refund policy does **not** apply to a verified, sustained failure on our
side. If a paid feature that depends on our service (for example, license
validation or the account/billing portal) is **materially unavailable due to a
Voxmelt-side outage exceeding 48 continuous hours within a single billing
cycle**, contact **help@voxmelt.com** and we'll issue a **pro-rated credit or
refund** for the affected portion of that cycle.

Note that the core local features - recording, transcription, and on-device AI
processing - run entirely on your machine and don't depend on our servers, so
they're not affected by our outages (and equally, problems with your own
hardware, drivers, or third-party model downloads aren't a Voxmelt-side
outage).

## 5. Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits any **non-waivable refund or withdrawal rights**
you have under the consumer law where you live. For example, EU/UK consumers
may have statutory withdrawal rights; where those apply and can't be waived,
they take precedence over §3. If you think you have such a right, email us and
we'll sort it out.

## 6. How refunds are paid

Where a refund or credit is issued, it's processed back to your **original
payment method through Razorpay**, our payment processor. Timing depends on
Razorpay and your bank.
