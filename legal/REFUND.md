# Voxmelt Refund Policy

**Version:** 3.0
**Effective date:** 18 August 2026
**Applies to:** One-time Pro and Studio purchases of Voxmelt.

---

## The short version

You get a **15-day full-feature trial, no card required**, so you can confirm
Voxmelt actually runs on your machine *before* you pay a cent. Because you can
fully evaluate every paid feature on your own hardware first, **purchases are
non-refundable.**

Voxmelt Pro and Studio are **one-time purchases**. You pay once and the licence
is yours permanently. There is no subscription, no renewal, no recurring charge,
and therefore nothing to cancel.

## 1. Try before you buy

Every new installation gets a **15-day Studio trial** with no payment details
required. During the trial you can run the full product on your own hardware:
your own microphone, your own CPU or GPU, your own accuracy expectations. That
is the whole point of a local-first application. Nothing is throttled, and no
card is charged when the trial ends - a purchase happens **only** when you
explicitly choose to buy.

We deliberately do not auto-charge at the end of the trial.

## 2. Purchases are non-refundable

Because the trial gives you a complete opportunity to evaluate every paid
feature on your own hardware before paying, **payments are non-refundable**.

By completing a purchase you confirm that you have had a full opportunity to
evaluate the software during the trial and that you accept this no-refund
policy.

## 3. What you get, and for how long

A Pro or Studio purchase grants a **perpetual licence** for that tier on the
device fingerprints associated with your account, as described in the
[Terms of Service](./TERMS.md). It does not expire and does not need renewing.

There is nothing to cancel. If you want to stop using Voxmelt, simply uninstall
it. Your local transcripts and settings live on your own machine and are never
sent to us, so uninstalling removes them along with the app.

## 4. Failed or incomplete payments

If a payment does not complete, no licence is granted and no charge is settled.
Your account stays on the free tier and your local data on the desktop app is
untouched. You are welcome to try the purchase again at any time.

If money left your account but your plan did not upgrade, email
**help@voxmelt.com** with the payment reference and we will fix it.

## 5. Chargebacks & disputes

If you believe a charge is incorrect or unauthorised, please email
**help@voxmelt.com** *before* raising a chargeback with your bank. We aim to
respond within 2 business days and resolve billing issues quickly. Raising a
chargeback without contacting us first may result in account termination and
revocation of the licence.

## 6. Your statutory rights

This no-refund policy does **not** override any **non-waivable** refund or
withdrawal rights you may have under the mandatory consumer law where you live.
Where such rights apply and cannot be waived, they take precedence. If you think
you have such a right, email us and we'll sort it out.

## 7. Contact

Billing questions: **help@voxmelt.com**
